Before recommending the self-serve gitops PR flow (`switch-role gitops-build` to author + `switch-role gitops-lead` to approve), confirm the peer is an *internal* lead — i.e., a lead whose runtime executes inside an in-cluster devbox pod with mounted `gh-gitops-build` and `gh-gitops-lead` token roles. *External* leads (Codex CLI on a workstation, runtimes outside the cluster) do not have those mounts and must use the courtesy-author + non-author-approver flow instead. The two GitHub App identities (alfred-worker-agent for build, alfred-lead-agent for lead) are what satisfy the same-actor branch protection gate — that mechanism only exists where both mounts are present.
