## Merge rule
The lead merges after a passed review (AP-1230, the `lead-review` ruleset).

## Scope
In: the alfred-agent plugin (the lead agreement, routed rules, specialist agents, skills, commands, hooks, config, bin scripts, templates, the bundled MCP config and tests). The shipped `doctrines/` is a copy.
Out: the catalog pin (alfred-cc-tools), platform ADRs and doctrines at their source (alfred-platform), and other leads' repos.
